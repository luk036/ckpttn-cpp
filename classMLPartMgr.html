◆ set_contraction_ratio()

void MLPartMgr::set_contraction_ratio ( double ratio)
inline

Sets the minimum required module-count reduction for a contraction.

A contraction is only accepted when the contracted hypergraph has fewer than hyprgraph.number_of_modules() / ratio modules, i.e. a reduction of at least ratio times. The default is 1.5 for binary partitioning and 1.05 for k-way (num_parts > 2).

Parameters
[in]ratioThe required reduction factor (must be at least 1; a value of 1 accepts any contraction that strictly reduces the module count).
